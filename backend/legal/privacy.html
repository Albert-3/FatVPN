Русская версия

FatVPN is a VPN service for mobile devices. This policy describes what data the app and its server process, why, and for how long. It is written to be checkable: everything below matches what the app's and the server's code actually do.

Data controller: Abgaryan Hakob
Data enquiries: suren.tamazyan05@gmail.com

1. The short version

We keep no logs of your activity. We do not record which sites you visit, which apps reach the network, what DNS queries your device sends, or anything about the contents of your traffic. We hold no data from which your internet activity could be reconstructed — and so there is nothing of that kind for us to hand to anyone, including anyone who demands it.

We do not sell or disclose your data to third parties — not for advertising, not for analytics, not for any other purpose. The app contains no ad networks, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party data-collecting SDKs. The only server the app talks to is our own.

2. What we do not collect

  • browsing history, destination IP addresses, DNS queries;
  • the contents of your traffic, in any form;
  • the list of installed apps (on Android the split-tunnelling screen needs it — it is read on the device, shown to you, and never sent anywhere);
  • location, contacts, photos, calendar, microphone, camera;
  • advertising identifiers (IDFA, Android Advertising ID) — the app never asks for them;
  • your name, address, phone number or government ID;
  • payment card details — see section 7.

3. What we do collect, and why

DataWhyKept for
An install identifier — a random value the app creates on first launch. It reaches the server only as a hash with a secret salt; the server never receives the original value and cannot recover it from the hash. To give one free trial per install and no more, and to count how many devices run on one key (the limit is 3). As long as the key or trial it belongs to exists.
Platform — the string ios or android. To hand back a configuration your OS can use. With the device record.
Your key code and its expiry, plus the subscription identifier in the VPN panel. To know whether you have access, and until when. While the key is valid, plus what accounting requires.
Your Telegram user ID — only if you connected through our bot. It is the number Telegram assigns to an account; we receive no name, phone number or username from Telegram. To tie your key to an account, so a renewal or a key change reaches the app. As long as the account exists.
Session tokens — stored only as hashes, with the times they were created, rotated and revoked. To keep you signed in without re-entering your key, and to be able to end a session instantly if a key is stolen or revoked. Up to 90 days from the last rotation; deleted immediately on sign-out.
The IP address of your connection to our server — in in-memory rate-limit counters and in web-server technical logs. Protection against brute force and automated abuse. Briefly. It is never linked to your internet activity and never used for profiling.
The volume of traffic (in bytes), metered by the VPN panel. To enforce plan limits. Within the billing period. No destinations and no contents are stored alongside it.

4. What stays on your device only

This data never leaves your phone on its own:

  • session tokens — in the OS secure store (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android);
  • your settings: chosen country, split-tunnelling rules, language;
  • the app's technical log, kept so a failure can be diagnosed. It is sent only when you press "Share diagnostics" yourself, and only where you choose to send it. Keys and credentials are stripped out of it first.

Signing out in the app deletes the tokens, the VPN configuration and the stored server choice from the device.

5. The VPN permission

To route your traffic through an encrypted tunnel, the app needs permission to create a VPN connection: VpnService on Android, a Network Extension on iOS. The system asks for your consent separately, and without it no tunnel is raised.

That permission technically allows the app to see the traffic passing through it. We use it solely to encrypt your traffic and carry it to the server you selected. We do not inspect it, record it, modify it, or insert advertising into it.

6. Who we share data with

Nobody — in the sense of selling, trading, or giving third parties access for their own purposes. Data is processed only by the service providers the service cannot run without:

  • Our hosting provider, whose servers run our backend and the VPN nodes;
  • Telegram — if you connect or buy a key through the bot, Telegram's own policy applies to you there;
  • Apple and Google — as app stores. They give us anonymous install statistics; we give them nothing about you.

We will disclose data on a lawful demand from a competent authority — but only what exists can be disclosed, and logs of your activity are not among it (section 1).

7. Payment

Buying and renewing access happens outside the app, in the Telegram bot. The app neither contains nor asks for payment details, and we never see or store card numbers: they are handled by the payment provider under its own terms. In the app you only enter the key you received.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep what section 3 lists for as long as your access needs it, and delete it once it is no longer needed. At any time you may:

  • sign out in the app — session data is wiped from the device immediately;
  • ask us to delete every record associated with you, by writing to suren.tamazyan05@gmail.com. We will act on the request within a reasonable time; after deletion the key stops working.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, to restrict processing, or to withdraw consent. Write to suren.tamazyan05@gmail.com and we will answer. If our answer does not satisfy you, you may complain to the data protection authority in Armenia.

10. Children

The service is not intended for or directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect children's data. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

11. Security

The app's connection to our server is protected with TLS. Install identifiers and session tokens are stored on the server only as hashes — a database in the wrong hands yields neither the original identifiers nor usable tokens. No method of transmission is completely secure, but we work from the premise that data which does not exist cannot be stolen, and so we do not collect what we do not need.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. For material changes — a new category of collected data, for instance — we will also show a notice in the app before they take effect.
